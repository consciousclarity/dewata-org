under development

The Balinese calendar view on this page is a stub. It is not a working
calendar and never claims a date-of-record. The working calendar is
served by the API at api.dewata.org/dsp/v0.1/calendar/date/{YYYY-MM-DD}
and rendered by the underlying engine.
