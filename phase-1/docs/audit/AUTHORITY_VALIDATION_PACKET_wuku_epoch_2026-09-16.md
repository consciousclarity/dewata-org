## Purpose

We request a narrow expert assessment of the Gregorian-to-Wuku phase used by
Dewata. We are not asking the reviewer to approve Dewata generally, to choose
between software projects, or to validate Saka/Sasih and ritual rules outside
this packet.

## The two observed mappings

| Gregorian date | Dewata mapping | Peradnya/Rust mapping |
|---|---|---|
| 1971-01-24 | Pawukon position 127 (1-based), Wuku index 19, Pon–Redite | Pawukon position 1 (1-based), Sinta, Paing–Redite |
| 1981-08-23 | Pawukon position 1 (1-based), Sinta, Paing–Redite | Pawukon position 85 (1-based), Langkir, Umanis–Redite |
| 2026-09-01 | Pawukon position 66 (1-based), Sungsang, Paing–Anggara | Pawukon position 150 (1-based), Uye, Umanis–Anggara |

The mappings differ by a constant 84 days, or 12 Wuku, on every one of 73,049
dates from 1900-01-01 through 2099-12-31. Saptawara, Triwara, and Sadwara agree
throughout because 84 is divisible by 7, 3, and 6. This technical regularity
does not establish cultural authority.

## Questions for the reviewer

1. For the calendrical convention used by Kalender Bali Digital, what Wuku,
   Pancawara, and Saptawara should be assigned to each of the three dates above?
2. Is either 1971-01-24 or 1981-08-23 recognized as a Sinta/Redite cycle anchor?
   If neither is suitable, what documented Gregorian anchor should be used?
3. Is the 84-day difference an accepted convention or lineage difference, or
   would one mapping be considered erroneous for public Balinese-calendar use?
4. Does the answer vary by desa adat, textual lineage, historical period, or
   another stated scope?
5. What citable authority supports the assessment (lontar title, published
   calendar, PHDI/customary determination, book edition and page, or another
   inspectable source)?
