# Customary and institutional terms

This section describes Balinese customary institutions and the
sign-off machinery that is a prerequisite for promoting a calendar
ruleset. Pages here cite their sources in the repository and **make
no claim of customary authority** while their status is
`pending_customary_review` or `customary_attestation_required`.

document is present in the repository (`README_EN.md`,

matches the page wording. **The status is not a customary stamp.**
